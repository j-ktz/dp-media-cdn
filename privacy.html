Privacy Policy

Last updated October 20, 2026.

Dead and Petty Scheduler is a private tool used only by its owner, John Katez, to manage his own YouTube channel, Dead & Petty History. It is not available to other users.

What it accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, it uploads videos the owner made, sets their titles, descriptions and publish times, and posts the owner's own first comment on those videos. It uses the YouTube Data API scopes for uploading videos and managing the owner's channel.

What it stores

It stores one Google sign-in token on the owner's own computer so scheduled uploads keep working. It does not collect, sell or share any personal data, and it does not read or store information about viewers or other users.

Removing access

The owner can remove the tool's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy.
